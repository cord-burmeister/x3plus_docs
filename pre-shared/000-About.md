# A DIY Journey into Robotics

This series is a personal chronicle of building the X3Plus robot—one screw, one wire, one late-night breakthrough at a time. It’s not a textbook. It’s a hands-on, heart-in project that grew out of curiosity, a love for making things, and the thrill of seeing ideas come to life.

Each book in the series focuses on a different stage of the X3Plus build: mechanical design, electronics, coding, testing, and even the inevitable troubleshooting. It’s written for fellow hobbyists, makers, and anyone who’s ever looked at a pile of parts and thought, *I wonder if I could build a robot out of this.*

X3Plus isn’t a commercial product—it’s a personal experiment turned passion project. And this series is here to share the lessons, missteps, and small victories along the way.

If you’ve ever built something just to see if you could, this book is for you. If you’ve ever felt that rush when a messy idea turns into a working prototype, you’re in the right place. X3Plus is my robot—but this series is for all of us who build for the joy of it.
